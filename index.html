Property & Renovation Advice

How We Work

Clear thinking before construction begins.

The strongest renovation outcomes come from defining the real problem, understanding the existing building and setting a realistic order of work.

1

Understand the property and objective

Clarify what you are trying to achieve, what is already known and where uncertainty remains.

2

Identify risks and dependencies

Consider hidden conditions, access, services, specialist input and the correct sequence of trades.

3

Set priorities and a practical scope

Separate essential work from upgrades and avoid spending money in the wrong order.

4

Deliver or connect the right team

Proceed with Sonsie Carpentry where appropriate, or identify where another specialist or registered practitioner is required.

Experience With a Bigger Purpose

Built from real renovation work in the Yarra Valley.

Daniel Sonsie is the director of Sonsie Carpentry and founder of Carpenters for Housing Reform Victoria.
